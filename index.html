About the firm

Built on 31 years inside the businesses we now advise

MH Associates was founded by Milind Herlekar after a career spent inside manufacturing and corporate finance teams rather than behind a desk removed from them. Twenty-six of his thirty-one years were spent with Exide Industries Ltd, India's largest lead-acid battery company, working closely with production floors, sales teams and the boardroom alike.

That ground-level view is the reason the firm exists. Small and mid-sized businesses are routinely advised by people who have never had to close a month-end, defend a cost audit, or explain a working-capital gap to an owner — and MH Associates was started to close that gap: one point of contact who has actually done the job, covering accounts, costing, audit, tax and HR under a single roof.

Milind Herlekar, M Com, ACMA Founder — MH Associates
