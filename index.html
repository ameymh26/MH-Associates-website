About the firm

Built on 30+ years inside the businesses we now advise

MH Associates was founded by CMA Milind Herlekar after a career spent with manufacturing, sales and corporate teams, starting from scratch and rising to the level of finance controller in one mid-sized manufacturing organisation with a market capitalisation of approx. INR 35,000 Cr.

That ground-level view is the reason the firm exists. Micro, small and mid-sized businesses are routinely advised by people who have never had to close a month-end, defend an audit, work out a product cost, set organisational objectives and KPIs, optimise available resources or explain a working-capital gap to an owner, and MH Associates was started to close that gap: one point of contact who has actually done the job, covering accounting, financial management, costing, audit, taxation, HR and compliance under a single roof.
